Elist E-Commerce Performance Analysis

Tools: SQL, Excel

Dataset: 108K orders (2019–2022), $28.1M total revenue across a global electronics retailer.

Business Problem: Post-pandemic revenue was declining — leadership needed to understand which product lines, regions, and customer segments were driving or dragging performance.

Approach: Analysed sales trends, refund rates, loyalty program enrollment, and regional revenue splits. Segmented by product category and geography to surface actionable patterns.

Key Insights: Pandemic-era surge of +104% (2020) followed by a 46% revenue drop by 2022. North America drives 51.8% of revenue. 82% of repeat customers are not enrolled in the loyalty program — a clear retention gap. Apple products show disproportionately high refund rates.

SuperStore Sales Performance Dashboard

Tools: Power BI, Excel, SQL

Dataset: ~10K orders (2014–2017) from a multi-location US retail chain across 4 linked tables (orders, customers, products, locations).

Business Problem: Which customer segments, product categories, and regions are most profitable — and where is the business bleeding margin?

Approach: Built a Power BI dashboard combining cohort analysis and RFM segmentation to uncover retention patterns and segment value. Cross-tabbed category profitability by region.

Key Insights: Only 6% of customers return after their first month. Loyal Customers represent 27% of the base but generate 47% of revenue. Technology = 36% of revenue and 50% of profit; Furniture = 32% of revenue but only 6% of profit. California leads all states (~20% of revenue).
